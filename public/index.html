Pilot Brief

  • WASD or arrows — thrust / rotate · click space to plot a course
  • Space lasers · M missile · 123 laser banks
  • [] weapon waveform · ;' shield waveform
  • C hail · V scan · T transport cargo
  • H / F10 hyperdrive · +- throttle (needs Hyperdrive Core, clear space)
  • Engineering sliders split reactor output across weapons, life support, engines, sensors, and four shield facings
  • Match waveforms to blunt incoming fire. Mismatch yours to punch through theirs.
  • Do not fly the Vortex Gate with Voidseed still in the hold.
