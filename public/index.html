Miner Brief

  • WASD or arrows — thrust / rotate · click space to plot a course
  • Shift boost · X brake · scroll to zoom
  • Space or hold CUT — mining laser. Beam grabs the nearest rock in front of you
  • V scan nearest rock · C hail station or PIP
  • T sell the hold at a yard (must be in dock range)
  • J / JUMP — lock a course to the yard gate, then jump when you are in the orange ring
  • M / MAP — galaxy chart. Systems and wormhole lanes. Click a linked star to lock its gate. Esc closes
  • Currency is Credits (CR). Yards pay posted rates per tonne
  • Hold size and cutter rate follow ship class. Haulers mine. Fighters can, just worse.
  • Ice, iron, titanium, platinum-family, then the magenta Ghost Vein in Helios
