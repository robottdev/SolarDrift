Captain Scott (self-proclaimed) and PIP cut rock for Credits. The mining laser is the only thing on the hull that still works on purpose.

Inspired by Solar Winds (James Schmalz / Epic MegaGames, 1993). Original story: Captain Scott and PIP. Graphics generated by Stellar Sprites. Not affiliated with Epic Games.

Miner Brief

  • WASD or arrows — thrust / rotate · click space to plot a course
  • Shift boost · X brake · scroll to zoom
  • Space mining laser (hold). Beam cuts the first asteroid in front of you
  • V scan nearest rock · C hail station or PIP
  • T sell the hold at Helios Anchorage (must be in dock range)
  • Currency is Credits (CR). The Anchorage pays posted rates per tonne
  • Hold is 24t. Ice, iron, titanium, then platinum-family, then the magenta Ghost Vein
